# Luminae — Complete Card Reference

> **Engine-building race to 15 Eminence.** Collect colored Affinities, forge Artifact cards for permanent bonuses, and summon Luminary patrons. First player to 15 Eminence (Lumens) wins; ties break on fewest purchased cards.

---

## Notation used in this document

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Flare** | Ruby affinity (internal key `ruby`) |
| **Continuum** | Sapphire affinity (internal key `sapphire`) |
| **Verdance** | Emerald affinity (internal key `emerald`) |
| **Abyss** | Onyx affinity (internal key `onyx`) |
| **Radiance** | Pearl affinity (internal key `pearl`) |
| **Singularity** | Flux affinity (internal key `flux`) — wild, appears in Luminary bonuses only |
| **Em** | Eminence awarded upon forging the card |
| **Bonus** | Permanent affinity bonus granted (reduces future card costs by 1 of that colour) |
| **Cost** | Affinities spent from your supply to forge the card (bonuses count as discounts) |

## Part I — Affinity Codex

*The six cosmic affinities that flow through the world of Luminae.*

---

### Radiance *(pearl)*
**Domain:** Clarity · Protection
**Colour:** Pale silver-white (#F2F5FF)

Radiance is the oldest light — not the fire of creation, but the glow that remains after fire has learned patience. It illuminates without burning, shields without caging. Radiance crystals form wherever starlight has pooled undisturbed for long enough to forget it was ever moving. Collectors of Radiance build engines of quiet inevitability: no single card feels decisive, but the total effect arrives like dawn — all at once, and impossible to stop.

*"She holds back the dark not with fire, but with patience."*

---

### Flare *(ruby)*
**Domain:** Energy · Passion
**Colour:** Ember-orange (#FF5A3C)

Flare is the roar at the centre of every ignition — stellar, volcanic, emotional. It does not persist; it *insists*. Flare crystals fracture easily and regenerate fast, making Flare engines explosive but fragile until properly reinforced. Players who master Flare surge ahead in the mid-game with high-Eminence cards, but must maintain their furnace or watch it cool.

*"One touch and the sky remembers it was once a furnace."*

---

### Continuum *(sapphire)*
**Domain:** Time · Order
**Colour:** Deep cerulean (#3D6BFF)

Continuum flows through all things that move in sequence — tide, thought, cause and effect. It is the affinity of patient systems: slow to accumulate, impossible to rush, devastating when fully channelled. Continuum cards tend toward high single-colour costs, rewarding specialisation. The deep-water theme runs throughout: pressure, silence, the long patience of things that wait at the bottom.

*"The sea does not rage. It simply rises."*

---

### Verdance *(emerald)*
**Domain:** Life · Growth
**Colour:** Living green (#2ECC71)

Verdance is the will of living things to persist and expand. It is not gentle — it is *insistent*, cracking stone, overwriting stone, outlasting stone. Verdance engines grow slowly but compound relentlessly. Many Verdance cards are inexpensive at Tier 1 and medium at Tier 2, creating ramp strategies where early investment pays enormous dividends late.

*"Roots sink through reality looking for soil."*

---

### Abyss *(onyx)*
**Domain:** Void · Gravity
**Colour:** Deep violet (#7B1FA2)

Abyss is the absence that pulls, the gravity that consumes, the silence between two thoughts. It does not destroy so much as *un-create*. Abyss crystals are heavy and cold, and Abyss cards often carry the highest raw power in their tier at the cost of unusual or steeply coloured requirements. Abyss strategies tend toward late-game dominance, spending the early rounds as quiet predators.

*"A bead that drinks the light around it."*

---

### Singularity *(flux)*
**Domain:** Wild · Rare
**Colour:** Molten gold (#FFC43D)

Singularity does not belong to any one thing. It is the energy at the edge of category, the moment before a law becomes an exception. Singularity does not appear in card costs — it is a wild affinity earned only through Luminary bonuses, usable as any other affinity when paying for cards. When Singularity flows into a player's engine it bends the rules of cost in ways no other affinity can.

*"The point at which all forces agree to stop making sense."*

## Part V — Luminaries *(12 patron entities)*

**Setup:** Each game activates `playerCount + 1` Luminaries drawn from the available pool.

**Claiming:** On your turn, if the total permanent bonuses across your forged cards meets a Luminary's requirements (one or more affinity thresholds), you may claim it — gaining the Eminence listed immediately.

**Living Affinity Bonus:** Starting the turn *after* you claim a Luminary, you gain +1 bonus toward your Luminary's active affinity on every card purchase. You can toggle the active affinity at any time (even off-turn) by clicking the Luminary portal card. Single-eligible Luminaries use their one affinity permanently; multi-eligible ones cycle with a ↻ badge.

---
