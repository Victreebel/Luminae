# LUMINAe Tier III Galactic Roster Review v1.0

> Historical implementation record. The subsequent [all-tier audit](LUMINAe_ALL_TIER_ARTIFACT_AUDIT_v1.0.md) supersedes conflicting names, functions, tier thresholds, Event facts, and two Tier III illustrations. Earlier source assets remain for provenance.


Date: 2026-09-28.

**Accepted direction:** Artifact tiers describe the operational reach of
mastered technology. Tier III should deliver complete galactic achievements.
Blueprints supply specialized synthesis knowledge; Manifested Projects are the
distinct working results, at any supported scale.

**Implementation status:** the twenty identities, practical functions, and
Event classifications below have been reviewed and implemented under the
accepted direction. New card artwork is saved and connected to the game. The
[implementation record](LUMINAe_TIER_III_GALACTIC_IMPLEMENTATION_v1.0.md) records
lineage adjustments, preserved mechanics, asset provenance, and verification.
Future Project examples remain draft concepts and introduce no new recipes.

The twenty existing IDs, printed costs, bonus Affinities, and Eminence remain
fixed. The four current first-pool Projects and their recipes remain unchanged.

## What Makes These Tier III

Each Artifact already supplies a useful interstellar capability when Forged.
Its accomplishment depends on working across multiple star systems, rather
than merely being a local object invented by an advanced civilization.
Galactic reach can span inhabited regions without claiming that every star in
the galaxy is occupied or centrally controlled.

The Forge compresses the long history of research, construction, coordination,
and adoption. These networks are not assembled instantaneously. Transit retains
travel time; computation and governance accommodate delayed messages and
unequal clocks. Local infrastructure has local power, material, and maintenance
requirements. No proposal adds bulk-matter teleportation, free energy,
retrocausality, or an all-knowing central authority.

A future Blueprint must specify an exact method for combining these already
useful capabilities into a distinct operation. Being larger, more beautiful,
or built from other technology is not sufficient. A functioning network can
contribute its service or output without being physically consumed.

## Distinctions Worth Preserving

- **Ignition Reliquary / Chronoflare Array:** sustained replacement power sources
  versus coordinated delivery of stellar energy. Artificial suns require fuel
  and containment; they do not restart exhausted stars without new resources.
- **Extinction Furnace / Star-River Crucible / Relic Forge Commons:** reclaimed
  feedstock, large-scale habitat production, and verified precision manufacture.
  None is merely a differently colored general-purpose factory.
- **Worldroot / Overgrowth / Necrobiome / Biosphere Concordance:** establishing
  ecosystems, sustaining living stellar habitats, restoring damaged substrates,
  and maintaining exchange between unlike ecologies.
- **Recursive Commonwealth / Galactic Concordance / Witness Constellation:**
  continuity within a diverging civilization, negotiated services between
  sovereign societies, and independent evidence custody. A witness station
  does not automatically govern, and a treaty institution cannot manufacture
  authentic consent.
- **Chronology Accord / Chronoflare Array:** reconciling past records versus
  coordinating active operations. Both involve time; only the latter function
  requires active distributed synchronization in the implemented Event facts.
- **Collapse Mandala:** route isolation stops particular failure pathways. It
  does not halt gravitational collapse, contain an entire supernova, or prevent
  every ecological or political disaster.

**Deliberate change from the old roster:** Starway Spine replaces Wormgate
Spine. The old migration-portal premise conflicts with the current Lore Bible.
The transit fantasy remains monumental through stellar launch infrastructure,
traveling fleets, navigation, and arrival systems.

## Implemented Event Classification Review

These classifications are implemented in `artifact-event-facts-v3`. Labels use the existing closed capability vocabulary, with one or two
capabilities per Artifact. Capabilities describe relevant responses; they are
not universal protection or new activated powers. Current Event rules still
determine the effect, eligible targets, operational state, and protections.

The dependency column evaluates only `dependency:distributed_synchronization`:
shared timing, aligned active control, or distributed correction state.
Having multiple sites, exchanging messages, or retaining records alone does
not qualify. An exclusion from this dependency is not immunity to other Events.

The new Tier III roster has six synchronization memberships. With the four
unchanged Tier I/II memberships, the complete catalog now has ten memberships
and eighty explicit exclusions. The previous catalog had five memberships.
Event cadence, weights, and effect rules remain unchanged; the implementation
record describes the changed eligibility and its balance implications.

Several changes are substantive rather than cosmetic: Relic Forge Commons
really fabricates, Collapse Mandala really isolates failures, and Dark-Sector
Aperture actually combines separated detectors. Their response tags
were re-reviewed against these functions rather than inherited from their
smaller previous identities.

When writing approved card copy, the practical sentence must expose the relevant
action or dependency. Keep the explicit tags beneath the lore and their expanded
explanations available. A mystery sentence must not contradict that explanation
or imply undeclared immunity, prophecy, or universal competence.

## Blueprint Separation And Name Collisions

The pre-redesign registry listed the earlier galactic names as future Project leads.
The restored concepts now identify Artifact achievements, and their duplicate
future Project leads have been removed throughout the Tier III registry. Do
not leave an Artifact pointing toward itself or toward the same operation under
a different label. This includes secondary leads on other cards, not just each
card's primary lead. Wormgate Spine must also be retired or explicitly revised.

This affects future concepts, not the implemented Antimatter Detonator,
Mantle-to-Orbit Foundry, Ascension Registry, or Worldshield Covenant. Their
recipes, public rules, and manifestation behavior are unchanged.

The following examples test the distinction. They are **draft synthesis
concepts, not new game rules, approved recipes, or an expansion commitment**.
The listed capabilities identify causal ingredients; exact recipe size and
balance still require a separate design decision.

| Draft Project | Distinct result and specialized synthesis | Why the proposed ingredients are indispensable |
|---|---|---|
| **Silent Exodus** | A protected evacuation operation moves dormant populations along concealed departure and arrival schedules without exposing their destination through the normal route network. Travel time remains real. | Starway Spine supplies transport; Cryptobiotic Constellation supplies viable dormant populations and receiving refuges; Ordered Silence conceals and authenticates the coordinated route instructions. The Blueprint specifies how dormancy, movement, and covert scheduling operate together. |
| **Causality Audit Court** | A treaty institution adjudicates disputes whose accounts disagree because witnesses experienced different clocks, then gives its rulings effect through agreed service-access rules. It can return an unresolved finding. | Chronology Accord constrains possible event order; Witness Constellation supplies independently authenticated testimony; Galactic Concordance supplies consent and enforcement. The Blueprint specifies evidentiary reconciliation and a valid path from uncertain records to a binding ruling. |
| **Last-Light Evacuation** | A staged rescue keeps a failing settlement habitable until replacement habitats are ready, then transfers its population before the old energy supply becomes unusable. | Ignition Reliquary provides temporary sustainable power; Star-River Crucible builds suitable replacement habitats; Starway Spine carries the population. These Projects contribute a particular integrated operation. Their ingredients
already work and remain impressive independently. They need not outsize every
Tier III Artifact to justify being Projects.

## Original Art And Migration Sequence

The following sequence records the implementation plan; completion evidence
is in the linked implementation record. Future Project examples are still drafts.

1. **Review this roster.** Resolve names, functional overlap, galactic reach,
   and physics before drafting final card prose or generating twenty images.
   New prose remains draft under the Dialogue Authorship Contract until approved.
2. **Check lineage and exact recipes.** Confirm each proposed function fits its
   fixed printed Affinity cost and lower-Tier developmental routes. Resolve
   future Project leads across the registry. Change the proposal if it cannot
   fit; do not silently rebalance costs, bonuses, Eminence, or existing recipes.
3. **Migrate each approved identity coherently.** Update the shared technology
   registry, effective server lore overrides, practical roles, forms, lineage,
   Archive and card-browser presentation, and depiction/deployment metadata.
   Replace ambiguous fallback text so an old component name cannot reappear.
4. **Re-review Events against those functions.** Update explicit capability and
   dependency facts, source evidence, reviewed exclusions, and affected semantic
   checks together. Verify forecast explanations and tags below the lore.
   Reassess targeting frequency where the new functions change eligibility.
5. **Approve a small representative art set before completing the roster.**
   Suggested subjects: Starway Spine, Stellar Overgrowth, Matrioshka Chorus,
   and Galactic Concordance. Together they test transit, biological engineering,
   computing megastructures, and civic infrastructure. Inspect full and compact
   mobile cards for a readable focal subject and distinct silhouettes.
6. **Finish art and verify lifecycle presentation.** Replace runtime WebPs and
   source references only after approval; inspect Forge, Archive, card detail,
   and Civilization depictions. Preserve the established cosmic refill and
   audio behavior. Check relevant card-data, Event, Blueprint, and presentation
   tests; packaging is outside this work.

Use visible activity and physical scale cues: ships, inhabited chambers,
construction fronts, worlds, radiators, collectors, and working exchange
interfaces. A local view may represent a distributed achievement, but its
function must remain evident. Avoid twenty similar galaxy maps, decorative
constellations, and miniature emblems with a nebula behind them. Card art and
the Civilization scene must agree about physical form and placement.

Tier II gets a targeted follow-up only where it leaves a specific progression
gap or still depicts an inadequate key or token. This document does not authorize
a blanket Tier II rewrite.
