# LUMINAe Production Balance Audit v2.0

## Status

This is the controlling human-readable evidence ledger for the production-rules
audit. The Domain Epoch laboratory is excluded from every conclusion below.

## Confidence-Ranked Findings

### Confirmed

1. **The equal-turn exhaustion correction fixes the unequal-turn defect in the
   measured control.** All 45,000 corrected-control games completed and none
   ended with unequal turns. Frontier exhaustion still ended 1 Quick 4-player,
   19 Standard 4-player, and 247 Epic 4-player games, but those endings now
   resolve after equal opportunity.
2. **Correcting exhaustion does not certify every seat cell.** Quick 3-player,
   Standard 3-player, Epic 3-player, and Epic 4-player corrected-control cells
   exceed the approved four-point opener-relative spread gate.
3. **Luminary initiative remains structural.** Corrected-control first-to-last
   access ratios exceed 1.25 in every 3- and 4-player cell. Starting Eminence
   may narrow winner differences, but it does not equalize access or agency.
4. **The late Artifact economy over-collapses in the control, but the corrected
   lineage candidate is not the answer yet.** The report distinguishes advanced
   zero-cost Forges backed by a valid Built On connection. Under the approved
   rule, that connection substitutes one requirement and waives the payment
   floor; every advanced zero-cost Forge in the lineage screen is therefore
   lineage-earned. The same waiver accelerates matches by roughly 5–6% instead
   of adding late-game friction, and no opportunity-normalized denominator yet
   proves a 50% reduction in otherwise free advanced Forges.
5. **Encrypt does not yet function as reliable development commitment.** In
   the corrected control, encrypted-to-Forge conversion is 6.43–7.52% in
   Quick, 11.14–12.44% in Standard, and 16.90–18.61% in Epic. Stranded reserves
   remain about 1.67–2.11 per player. These are aggregate adaptive-bot results;
   the required development-policy-specific conversion is still unreported.
   Exact-semantics Focus materially improves aggregate conversion to
   `21.80 / 19.51 / 20.02%` in Quick, `30.39 / 27.46 / 26.13%` in Standard,
   and `39.07 / 35.55 / 33.61%` in Epic for 2/3/4 players respectively. Quick
   3-player remains just below the 20% aggregate threshold, and aggregate
   conversion does not substitute for the policy-specific gate.
6. **The current civilization arc is format-sensitive.** The timing metric now
   defines “near the ending” as first attainment during the final quarter of
   observed turns. Corrected-control Standard winners reach their first Tier III
   in that window in 80.82%, 80.24%, and 72.70% of 2/3/4-player games, inside
   the 70–90% target. Epic winners reach their second Tier III in that window in
   only 80.24%, 78.52%, and 71.56%, so every Epic cell misses its 90% target.
7. **Winner scoring is primarily Artifact-based, with meaningful Luminary
   bursts.** Across corrected-control cells, winner Artifact averages range
   from 11.50 to 21.49 Eminence, Luminary-arrival averages from 3.83 to 6.33,
   and starting compensation from 0.49 to 1.00. These are descriptive source
   averages, not estimates of causal scoring power.
8. **Card-bound Focus has an unavoidable 3-player deadlock.** Under the exact
   choice semantics, 3-player play completes only 993 of 1,000 games in every
   format, with seven max-turn stalls per cell. Replay diagnosis reaches the
   same terminal condition: full hands, full reserve capacity, and an empty
   natural Well leave no legal action that can restore circulation. The
   improved commitment rate does not cure this rules defect.
9. **Neither Blueprint slot model passes certification.** Across all nine cells
   per variant, the two-slot control manifests in 27.7–52.8% of matches and has
   a 13.32–23.38-point largest per-Blueprint manifestor-versus-nonmanifestor
   delta. The one-slot variant manifests in 28.2–45.7% and has a 9.31–21.45-point
   largest delta. Both
   complete 100% of games with zero unequal turns and zero secrecy failures,
   but every cell fails both the 10–20% manifestation and at-most-two-point
   manifestor-power gates. Opener spread varies by cell and is not the universal
   reason for rejection.
10. **The automated candidate shortlist is empty.** Every 1,000-game candidate
   family fails at least one hard gate. No candidate is eligible for 5,000-game
   finalist certification or paired creator comparison yet. The current
   integrated bundle is discarded rather than carried forward as a finalist.
11. **Nonexclusive Luminary contact solves access but still misses a Standard
    arc cell.** It completes 100% of games, remains inside the accepted pacing
    band, and holds first-to-last access to 1.002–1.219 in all nine cells.
    Standard near-ending attainment is 87.0%, 90.2%, and 91.2% for 2/3/4
    players; the 4-player cell exceeds the 70–90% band. Its
    mixed-policy 4-player Quick and Standard opener spreads are 6.9 and 5.3
    points respectively; those are screening observations, not parity
    certification.

### Provisionally healthy

- The 90-Artifact catalog and natural Well quantities remain broadly neutral
  under the tested control. This does not establish strategy diversity because
  the 5,000-game certification control used only the adaptive policy.
- Printed Affinity demand is asymmetric, but available winner bonus shares do
  not justify card-by-card cost changes before system candidates are resolved.
- Completion is 100% in every corrected-control cell. Completion is not the
  current bottleneck; parity, strategy-policy balance, Luminary access, Encrypt
  conversion, and advanced-cost provenance are.

### Evidence limitations

- The former general simulator remains exploratory and is not authoritative.
- The 5,000-game control uses adaptive bots only. It cannot certify the
  cross-policy gate.
- The Artifact reports do not isolate Encrypt conversion by
  development-oriented policy. Their zero-cost provenance supports the lineage
  result above, but broader opportunity-normalized comparisons still require a
  stable denominator.
- “Near the ending” is now an explicit instrumentation choice: first attainment
  during the final 25% of observed decision turns. It is a useful pacing proxy,
  not proof that the milestone felt climactic to a human player.
- Blueprint actual-manifestor outcomes are post-manifestation associations, not
  causal effect estimates. Assembly itself selects for particular game states.
- Card-owner win association is likewise selection-biased and is not causal
  power evidence.

Finish reasons are explicitly classified as `frontier_exhaustion`: the

Epic. Every other cell has zero `frontier_exhaustion` finishes, every cell has
zero `other` finishes, and all exhaustion endings preserve equal turns with
zero violations.

Some individual cells pass the four-point opener-spread gate and others do not.
Neither configuration is certifiable because **every** cell misses the
manifestation window, and every cell has at least one Blueprint outside the
two-point manifestor-versus-nonmanifestor power bound. The pooled association
sometimes falls inside two points, but that cannot conceal a per-Blueprint
outlier. These are post-manifestation associations rather than causal effect
estimates. Reducing each civilization to one private Blueprint does not solve
either failure. This comparison rejects both configurations for Competitive
Blueprint enablement; it does not decide whether Blueprints belong in authored
asymmetric scenarios.

## Automated Gates

- completion at least 99.5%;
- zero unequal-turn endings;
- opener-relative spread no greater than four percentage points in every
  equal-policy certification cell; mixed-policy screening does not certify
  this gate;
- pacing within 10% of the corrected control unless format length is the tested
  variable;
- no strategy policy more than five normalized win points above the field;
- Luminary first-to-last access ratio no greater than 1.25 without historical
  score compensation;
- 70–90% of Standard winners and at least 90% of Epic winners first attain the
  defined Tier III milestone during the final quarter of observed turns;
- at least 50% fewer unearned zero-cost advanced Forges;
- at least 20% encrypted-to-Forge conversion for development-oriented bots and
  fewer than 1.5 stranded reserves per player; and
- Blueprint manifestation 10–20%, pooled and per-Blueprint
  manifestor-versus-same-cell-nonmanifestor deltas within two points, and no
  completion or secrecy regression.

Failure preserves the corrected control. A numerical pass would not authorize a
production migration without creator playtesting.

## Campaign Audit Boundary

Only two playable campaign-like surfaces currently exist: the fixed 4-player,
15-Eminence Architect Record qualifying match and the authored Lumii Blueprint
clearance scenario. Later Chronicles are documents, not implemented systems,
and receive no fabricated balance verdict.

The qualifying match hardcodes the old short format and one Hard-AI policy. It
must be re-evaluated after the Standard core is selected. The authored scenario
is not certified by the symmetric Blueprint comparison. See
`LUMINAe_IMPLEMENTED_CAMPAIGN_BALANCE_AUDIT_v1.0.md` for the evidence-ranked
campaign audit.

## Release Posture

- The equal-turn frontier-exhaustion correction is the only default correction
  authorized during this audit.
- No experimental family or integrated bundle is selected for migration.
- Experimental rulesets create process-local guest development rooms; their
  room, player, and game state never enter database or account history.
- Creator-only evidence can eventually select a development direction. It
  cannot by itself certify Competitive Blueprints for public release.
