# LUMINAe Tutorial Dialogue Audit v1.0

## Purpose

This is an exchange-level review surface for the current tutorial. It records
where the player's language is already protected and where live wording remains
a draft. It intentionally proposes no replacement dialogue.

Runtime copy is not a lore authority. Canon and disclosure are checked against
the Lore Bible, Story Mode Framework, Onboarding Doctrine, and Concordance.

## Highest-Risk Drafts

### G: First Forge And Civilization Inspection

**Intent:** The player should understand that the card is LUMINAe's readable
path, the paid Affinities return, and the civilization retains a lasting
capability represented by `+1 Verdance`. The optional detour should show the
actual civilization and Replication Spore's in-world form.

**Why it needs review:** The current explanation and the in-scene guidance were
written during implementation. They are accurate but carry more explanatory
weight and specificity than the surrounding approved voice.

### H: Encryption And Singularity

**Intent:** Direct players learn what Encrypt does and perform it. Inquisitive
players may ask why Lumii cannot do it, earning the authority discovery.
Singularity is finite interface capacity and a wildcard, not a sixth natural
Affinity or energy from the Architect's universe.

**Why it needs review:** The optional answer currently repeats the distinction
across four declarative lines. The information hierarchy is sound; the cadence
has not been approved.

### I: Eminence And Luminaries

**Intent:** Teach 15-Eminence closing, equal turns, permanent-Affinity pattern
eligibility, Luminary agency, and the fact that the arrival is a projection.

**Why it needs review:** Seven mandatory lines introduce several ideas before
the demonstration. This is the clearest remaining place where instructional
completeness may be crowding natural conversation.

### J: Final Debrief

**Intent:** Make clear that the practice civilization was simulated, meeting
Lumii was real, a real match records history, and Lumii remembers it.

**Why it needs review:** These are important launch promises, but all four lines
are currently implementation-authored. Their meaning should remain; their voice
has not been accepted.

## Review Method

Review one group at a time. The player can approve an intent, add one voice
rule, replace a representative exchange, or lock the group. Only affected draft
lines should then be shown. Locked neighboring copy stays untouched.

Recommended order: `G`, `H`, `I`, `J`, then the lower-risk bridges in `C`, `E`,
and `F`.
