# LUMINAe Mechanic-To-Lore Mapping v1.0

## Purpose

This document states what each core mechanic changes in real history and what
part of its presentation is LUMINAe interface compression. It is fiction
doctrine, not an authorization to change current rules.

It explains, but does not supersede, the Lore Bible's ontology, Technology
System v2's Artifact semantics, or Blueprint First Pool v2's exact Project
rules.

## Core Match Loop

| Mechanic | Literal historical change | Interface compression |
|---|---|---|
| Create match | LUMINAe begins observing 2–4 real civilizations already interacting within one Domain. | Lobby, seats, seed, and synchronized start. |
| New match Domain | Observation normally moves to a causally separate Domain. | A generated Domain identifier and new board. |
| Turn | One civilization completes a consequential interval of research, governance, coordination, and construction. | A discrete player action lasting seconds. |
| Well reservoir | A finite amount of each Affinity mode is temporarily addressable and uncommitted within the Domain. Affinity is addressability, not fuel. | Colored integer supply. |
| Harness three | A civilization stabilizes a broad alignment across three modes. | Taking three differently colored tokens. |
| Harness two | A civilization concentrates one sufficiently available mode. | Taking two matching tokens only above the printed supply condition. |
| Holding cap | The civilization cannot coherently sustain unlimited unresolved development alignments. | Ten-token cap and mandatory returns. |
| Pay Affinity | Stabilized alignments are committed to one accelerated development program. | Moving tokens from holdings to the Well. |
| Return to Well | The program becomes operational and releases its temporary claim on Domain addressability. | Paid tokens immediately refill reservoirs. |
| Artifact cost | The program requires particular physical, informational, biological, and institutional disciplines. | Printed colored quantities. |
| Artifact bonus | Operational mastery creates durable local expertise, infrastructure, and institutions. | Permanent cost reduction in one mode. |
| Eminence | The civilization's actions become more consequential and legible during the observed epoch. | Victory points printed on cards and effects. |

## Forge And Technology

| Mechanic | Literal historical change | Interface compression |
|---|---|---|
| Face-up Forge | The cohort has a shared comparative frontier of currently accelerable technological trajectories. | Twelve cards in three rows. |
| Archive deck | Further opportunities are not yet consequential or legible at the current frontier. | Concealed tier deck and randomized order. |
| Forge Artifact | Architect influence helps one civilization reach operational mastery earlier than it otherwise would; the civilization still performs and governs the work. | Purchase, confirmation, animation, and tableau placement. |
| Artifact signature | The class remains universally recognizable despite local embodiments. First mastery creates Domain-local signature interference for that class. | One canonical card identity with civilization-specific implementation art and consequences. |
| Remove claimed card | Same-class signature interference closes the accelerated first-mastery window, often for centuries or the epoch. Related technologies and lineages remain available. | One unique card leaves the shared row. |
| Refill Forge | The next reachable possibility becomes legible; no idea changes intellectual ownership. | Draw the next tier card. |
| Built On / Leads Toward | Capabilities have genuine intellectual and infrastructural lineage. | Detail metadata; not a standard prerequisite unless a Chronicle says otherwise. |
| Blueprint | A civilization understands a synthesis relation among several capabilities. | Recipe or private Blueprint card. |
| Manifest Project | The synthesis is built as an operational device, institution, organism, infrastructure, or network. | Blueprint activation and public Project state. |
| Operational Reach | The civilization demonstrates capability at planetary, stellar, or galactic scale. | Match milestone derived from a calibrated breadth of implementations. |
| Kardashev Type | Sustained energy use, infrastructure, and physical extent change over historical time. | Separate metadata; never triggered by one Forge or free cost. |

## Encrypt And Controllers

| Mechanic | Literal historical change | Interface compression |
|---|---|---|
| Encrypt | An Architect isolates an uncommitted acceleration pathway from rival Architects' current view without creating a mastery signature or preventing eventual discovery. | Move a public or concealed card to private storage. |
| Encrypt cap | Only limited private intervention bandwidth can remain unresolved. Foundry-bound components are controlled archived pathways, not ordinary Encrypt actions. | Three ordinary Encrypted cards plus a separately labeled Foundry Components group. |
| Singularity | Provisional: finite Architect-only LUMINAe capacity may substitute for one natural-mode requirement. | Portable wildcard reward; retained only as the Focus-test baseline. |
| Focus candidate | A private pathway is bound to one printed natural mode rather than producing a universal wildcard. | Later experimental Encrypt reward; not canon or production behavior yet. |
| Autonomous controller | A civilization develops through its own institutions without Architect-style presentation manipulation. | AI policy that cannot Encrypt. |
| Architect emulation | Lumii/LUMINAe applies a non-person decision model resembling Architect intervention to a real civilization. | Standard AI with the complete Architect action set. |

## Luminaries And Hostile Effects

| Mechanic | Literal historical change | Interface compression |
|---|---|---|
| Meet Luminary requirement | A civilization's durable Affinity pattern forms a stable, compatible address. | Permanent-bonus threshold. |
| Automatic standard alliance | Contact, terms, and mutual acceptance occur during the compressed interval. | Immediate mechanical acquisition. |
| Chronicle acceptance/refusal | A Luminary and civilization negotiate a consequential relationship. | Dialogue, terms, choice, and scenario branch. |
| Immediate contested claim | The first stable qualifying signal receives the Luminary's finite anchor attention in that Domain epoch. | Current turn-order resolution. |
| Luminary facet | One underlying person maintains an account-bounded relationship and memory. | Persistent account relation; other accounts remain sealed. |
| Burn | An unmastered acceleration pathway collapses during the observation epoch. | Card enters the Burn pile or recurrence path. |
| Assimilate | Final Hunger consumes an unmastered pathway into Affinity without creating a normal implementation. | Card leaves its pathway and produces the authored Affinity result. |
| Nullify | A pathway loses projected historical consequence or synthesis value without losing its practical capability. | Marker suppresses Eminence, Blueprint eligibility, or Encryption. |
| Damage | An operational implementation ceases functioning until repaired; mastery and signature interference persist. | Disabled implementation state and repair condition. |
| Annihilate | An operational or nearly operational implementation is physically destroyed; discovery survives and the class remains unavailable this epoch. | Permanent removal from play, reserved for exceptional weapons. |
| Foundry seal | Controlled decommissioning archives mastery, removes the operational bonus, unwinds active interference, and binds the pathway in Cipher storage for re-Forge. | Move the three components to the separate Foundry Components group without granting Singularity or consuming ordinary Encrypt capacity. |

## Match Closure And Records

| Mechanic | Literal historical change | Interface compression |
|---|---|---|
| Reach Eminence target | The observed epoch reaches a useful historical closure point. | Trigger the final round at the configured score. |
| Equal-turn completion | LUMINAe closes observation after equivalent final intervals for every civilization. | Finish the current round. |
| Winner | One civilization had the greatest observed consequence during the epoch. | Highest Eminence plus any interface-only initiative adjustment. |
| Loss | A civilization ends the observation epoch with lower consequence. It normally continues to exist and develop. | Defeat result; no implied conquest or extinction. |
| Standard record | A real cohort history is preserved without authored campaign consequences. | Domain, epoch, controllers, final states, account access, and cohort summary. |
| Chronicle primary outcome | The first authored result becomes irreversible account canon. | Persistent outcome, flags, relationship state, and later consequences. |
| Archive Rehearsal | Lumii and the Architect knowingly observe a simulation of an alternate path. | Replay record that never overwrites primary history. |
| Calibration Insight | The Architect demonstrates optional preparedness and receives clearer risk information. | One exact Threshold protocol or forecast is unredacted. |
| Threshold access | Three primary Chronicle outcomes complete mutual interface calibration. | Campaign unlock independent of wins and Insights. |

## Interpretation Rule

When a future mechanic cannot be mapped to a historical change without making
ordinary technology magically exclusive, erasing civilizational agency,
creating free energy, introducing retrocausality, or treating Affinity as moral
judgment, mark it as a concordance conflict. Do not repair it with new cosmology
until redesign and balance alternatives have been considered.
