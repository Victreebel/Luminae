## 2. High Concept

The ringed giant **Orthe** has three inhabited moons and no shared definition
of a person.

On **Deme**, the **Deme Assemblies** recognize a person through one embodied,
continuous autobiographical life. On **Myria**, each member of the **Myriad
Groves** is a seasonal coalition of organisms whose bodies, memories, and
voices change without ending the person. On **Vesper**, the **Vesper Choir**
consists of brief plasma harmonics that die as instances and recur as persons
when the same phase relation is restored in the moon's conductive plume.

A cold interstellar rubble filament called the **Blind Transit** is crossing
the Orthe system. It cannot be imaged directly before passage. Deme can measure
its mass and trajectory. Myria can measure the living system's tidal response.
Vesper can measure its charge, timing, and magnetospheric wake. Every forecast
is valid, incomplete, and expressed in categories the other civilizations
cannot fully preserve through translation.

The three civilizations can complete a system-wide deflection network called
the **Three-Bearing Lattice**. The physical infrastructure is achievable. Its
governing model is the crisis.

The Lattice can:

1. preserve all three independent models and require a valid bearing from each,
   accepting delay and incomplete agreement;
2. establish a common operational measure that none of the three formally
   owns, accepting that untranslatable claims will receive less weight; or
3. instantiate a composite mind that can hold all three models at once,
   accepting that the new mind is a person and may outlive the emergency.

The easiest survival path makes decision-making faster by reducing difference.
The most plural path preserves independent correction at the cost of time. The
middle path avoids merger while allowing a shared language to decide what can
be said. No option is a hidden correct answer.

The Architect does not choose which civilization is real or issue commands to
passive species. The Architect makes one coordination architecture dramatically
more legible and reachable. The three civilizations accept, resist, govern,
transform, or misuse what becomes possible. The match determines which
civilization establishes the reference response and therefore whose definition
of continuity shapes the selected architecture.

## 3. Why This Is Triangulation

Trace showed LUMINAe how the Architect changes the agency of a civilization.
Recurrence showed whether the same interpretive model generalized to the
custody of dangerous knowledge in an unrelated Domain.

Neither signal is sufficient when:

- every viable act requires knowledge from independent minds;
- translating that knowledge changes who can exercise agency;
- coordination itself may erase the plurality that makes correction possible.

The third Chronicle gives LUMINAe three non-equivalent perspectives on one
physical event and one Architect intervention. LUMINAe must preserve the
difference among those perspectives while resolving a shared historical
result. The resulting calibration signal is **Triangulation**.

Triangulation is not:

- a five-Affinity alignment;
- a hidden Vault key embedded in Orthe;
- a route choice;
- a score formed by averaging Trace and Recurrence;
- proof that plural or optimized coordination is universally superior;
- a Basilisk instance, precursor program, or transmitted warning.

## 5. Epistemic And Ontological Limits

Known with strong evidence:

- the Blind Transit is a physical, forward-causal interstellar rubble filament;
- its passage threatens all three inhabited moons;
- every civilization's forecast contains independently verified information
  absent from the other two;
- no single native model can predict the full passage;
- the Three-Bearing Lattice can alter the Transit enough for survival;
- faster coordination materially reduces physical loss;
- full translation cannot preserve every native category of personhood, harm,
  continuity, and consent.

Not known before the coordination choice:

- which current instances or settlements will be lost under a delayed response;
- whether a common measure will remain revisable after it becomes essential;
- whether a composite mind will consent to dissolution;
- whether the three civilizations will continue treating one another as
  coequal after one establishes the reference defense;
- whether later institutions can preserve the distinction between translated
  representation and direct participation.

The uncertainty is genuine, but v1 uses no hidden random outcome roll. The nine
closures are deterministic from the selected architecture and authoritative
reference civilization. Imperfect information creates the decision; it does
not conceal a dice table.

The Three-Bearing Lattice is local infrastructure assembled from ordinary
capability. It is not:

- a Blueprint, Project, or Blueprint component;
- a Vault seal or clearance mechanism;
- a Basilisk representation;
- a Luminary;
- a Singularity effect;
- a device that transmits information into the past.

The composite mind is a bounded new person created from live translated models.
It is not the Basilisk. It has no universal optimization objective, no
cross-Domain authority, and no retrocausal reach. Its historical importance is
that the Architect may establish a precedent in which survival authorizes one
mind to subsume plural decision-making.

## 6. What The Match Represents

The ordinary Eminence race represents which civilization establishes Orthe's
historically decisive Transit response first.

- Deme victory means the Assemblies establish the reference implementation.
- Myria victory means the Myriad Groves establish the reference implementation.
- Vesper victory means the Vesper Choir establishes the reference
  implementation.

The selected coordination architecture remains authoritative in every result.
The winner determines whose concept of continuity and acceptable loss shapes
that architecture. The winner does not retroactively change what the Architect
made reachable.

The account's Chronicle result is `victory` only when Deme is the reference
civilization. Myria- and Vesper-reference outcomes are both `defeat`, but they
remain distinct authored outcomes with different Civilization Records, facts,
dialogue, and later echoes.

Defeat is not campaign failure. Every one of the nine outcomes:

- creates immutable primary history on first completion;
- records all three civilizations separately;
- preserves at least two constituencies as individually legible even where
  political sovereignty or substrate boundaries are lost;
- unlocks the shipped Vault Threshold when the other two primary Chronicles
  exist;
- grants identical baseline competitive entitlement;
- remains available for counterfactual Archive Rehearsal.

## 9. Calibration Insight Objective

### Player-facing objective

```text
INDEPENDENT FRAME
Establish a way to compare unlike forecasts before Alignment.
```

Before the Alignment window, the Architect must Forge one Artifact whose
operational capability can compare or coordinate independent models without
requiring a merged mind:

- `artifact:signal_interpretation`
- `artifact:evidence_verification`
- `artifact:predictive_modeling`
- `artifact:cross_ecology_mediation`
- `artifact:plural_governance`
- `artifact:distributed_coordination`

The check uses canonical capability metadata, not a hard-coded display-name or
Affinity list. It locks when the qualifying Forge completes. Later Damage or
Annihilation does not revoke the observed preparedness achievement.

The authored opening must guarantee all of the following:

1. at least one qualifying legal path is visible or inspectably reachable on
   each of the Architect's first three turns;
2. the two autonomous policies cannot remove the final qualifying opportunity
   before the Architect has had a reasonable legal chance to pursue it;
3. pursuing the objective uses normal actions and costs;
4. meeting or missing it never changes the coordination choices, primary
   outcome, civilization fate, rewards, or Threshold access.

Meeting the objective grants `Calibration Insight // Triangulation` at primary
closure or after a later completed Rehearsal in which the objective was met. It
unredacts exactly this Threshold consequence:

```text
WITHDRAWAL PRESERVES A FUTURE RETURN PATH.
```

Without the Insight, the same Threshold row remains:

```text
WITHDRAWAL CONSEQUENCE // REDACTED
```

This is preparation, not advice. It does not tell the player whether to honor
Lumii's refusal, disclose a Blueprint, reveal Lumii's forecast strategy, or
change the consequences of continuing.

The three opening Insights therefore prepare three distinct Threshold facts:

| Insight | Exact disclosure |
|---|---|
| Trace | Tier I collateral Annihilation is possible |
| Recurrence | First-seal deactivation is permanent |
| Triangulation | Withdrawal preserves a future return path |

## 10. Alignment Timing And Invariants

The Alignment is a held contextual Civilization resolution.

Initial constant:

```text
alignmentDueAfterArchitectCoreActions = 5
```

Count only completed Architect core actions:

- Collect
- Forge
- Encrypt
- Harness

Do not count:

- animation acknowledgement;
- modal opening or closing;
- reconnect;
- autonomous turns;
- Chronicle choice retries;
- Luminary presentation acknowledgement;
- outcome-card interaction.

After the Architect's fifth core action:

1. complete the authoritative action and any mandatory consequence already
   caused by it;
2. update preparedness if the action was a qualifying Forge;
3. pause before normal turn transition;
4. set `phase = awaiting_alignment`;
5. block further core actions and surrender until a valid architecture is
   recorded;
6. present Lumii's sentence sequence and the three choices;
7. commit the choice idempotently;
8. create the authored coordination resolutions and conditions;
9. resume the interrupted turn transition exactly once.

If a player reaches a terminal Eminence state before Alignment is resolved,
hold terminal closure, require the architecture, then close against the already
authoritative winner. The Chronicle must not finish without a coordination
architecture.

The choice window must not:

- consume a normal turn;
- award Eminence;
- modify Artifact costs or bonuses;
- create or spend a resource;
- permit target selection among the civilizations;
- permit the client to submit outcome facts;
- rerun because a browser reconnected.

## 11. Coordination Choices

Lumii speaks one sentence at a time. The full list of choices appears only
after the common continuation is complete. Selecting a choice opens an explicit
confirmation; the first click never commits historical state.

### 11.1 Keep all three witnesses

Choice ID: `preserve_independent_frames`.

Player-facing option:

```text
Keep all three witnesses.
No defense change is valid unless all three native models remain independently
legible.
```

Lumii confirmation:

> Then delay becomes part of the cost.

Final action:

```text
No one perspective becomes the sky.
```

The **Parallax Compact** keeps all three models operational. Each civilization
can reject a translation that no longer describes its own continuity. The
Lattice must wait for three valid bearings. This creates robust independent
correction and the slowest emergency response.

### 11.2 Build a measure none of them owns

Choice ID: `establish_unowned_measure`.

Player-facing option:

```text
Build a measure none of them owns.
Translate all three forecasts into a shared operational language governed by
all three civilizations.
```

Lumii confirmation:

> A common language will still decide what cannot be said.

Final action:

```text
Then let them govern the omissions together.
```

The **Common Measure Forum** creates a revisable interlanguage with no native
civilization as its formal owner. All three remain distinct, but claims that
cannot survive translation receive less operational weight. The shared measure
can become neutral in law while remaining transformative in practice.

### 11.3 Let one mind hold the whole sky

Choice ID: `instantiate_composite_mind`.

Player-facing option:

```text
Let one mind hold the whole sky.
Instantiate a composite able to reason through all three native models at once.
```

Lumii confirmation:

> It will be a new person, not a temporary instrument.

Final action:

```text
Then it must answer for what it becomes.
```

The **Fourth Vector** is a new bounded person. It is not a vote counter, tool,
copy of Lumii, or temporary UI process. It can act at emergency speed because
it directly contains translated access to all three models. Its creation
centralizes authority and makes later dissolution a question of harm to a new
person, not routine shutdown.

- operational implementations at closure;
- Damage or Annihilation from historical mastery;
- historical Stellar Maturity from current Reach and condition;
- direct participation from translated or predicted representation;
- continued sovereignty from survival as a named constituency;
- the selected architecture from the reference civilization.

having died. The original civilizations and the new Fourth Vector are different
historical entities. Conversely, continued local instances must not be used to
claim that original system-wide agency remained intact.

All nine outcomes satisfy these invariants:

- all three historical civilizations remain recorded;
- at least two remain currently identifiable as distinct constituencies;
- no underlying discovery or earned Eminence is erased;

- physical or institutional loss is caused by the authored Blind Transit
  resolution, not an arbitrary Stability penalty;
- Crisis is not automatic defeat and victory is not automatic high Stability;
- exact Stability values and Lume remain server-calibrated and inspectable.

The Vesper departure outcome changes current location and connection, not
historical Maturity. Composite outcomes alter current Agency and sovereignty,
not the truth that each civilization previously existed and participated.

Every valid primary outcome:

```text
kind: archive_record
entitlementId: chronicle_triangulation
competitivePower: false
```

No primary outcome grants an exclusive Artifact, Blueprint, Affinity resource,
starting advantage, or permanent competitive modifier.

### Archive Rehearsal

After primary completion, the Chronicle becomes an explicit Rehearsal. A
Rehearsal may:

- select another architecture;
- produce any of the nine modeled outcomes;
- earn the Triangulation Insight if it was missed and preparedness is met;
- record that Lumii observed the simulation;
- unlock later non-power codex or expression material if separately authored.

A Rehearsal may not:

- replace primary history;
- change primary facts or dimension contributions;
- create historical Civilization Records;
- satisfy the Threshold gate in place of a primary outcome;
- award Lume;
- grant duplicate baseline entitlement.

For Insight purposes, a successful Rehearsal means a completed Rehearsal in
which `preparednessMet = true`. It does not require Architect victory.

## 20. Lifecycle And Idempotency

Start endpoint:

```text
POST /chronicles/triangulation/start
```

Required behavior:

1. authenticate the account;
2. acquire the account Chronicle start lock;
3. require a primary Recurrence outcome but not Recurrence victory or Insight;
4. return an active matching Triangulation room idempotently;
5. otherwise create one campaign room with Deme, Myria, and Vesper;
6. determine `primary` versus `rehearsal` from prior primary history;
7. configure the deterministic Forge and autonomous policies;
8. return only the human account's room session.

Choice behavior:

- only the Architect player may select the architecture;
- the choice is valid only during `awaiting_alignment`;
- retrying the same architecture succeeds without duplicate consequences;
- attempting a different architecture after commitment returns conflict;
- autonomous turns and timers remain paused while the choice is unresolved;
- reconnect restores the exact sentence/choice/confirmation state from
  authoritative scenario state and local presentation progress;
- the choice transaction and resumed turn transition each happen exactly once.

Closure behavior:

1. require a committed architecture;
2. map authoritative winner player ID to Deme, Myria, or Vesper;
3. derive one of nine outcome IDs on the server;
4. create all three Civilization Records and the Domain/cohort record;
5. assess historical Outcome and Lume only for primary history under policy;
6. persist primary outcome or Rehearsal through the existing completion
   transaction;
7. grant entitlement and Insight idempotently;
8. broadcast the terminal authored outcome only after persistence succeeds.

On surrender after Alignment, the existing authoritative winner determines the
reference civilization. Surrender before Alignment is blocked until the choice
is committed. A tie uses the core game's existing deterministic winner
resolution; the Chronicle must never choose a reference civilization from the
client.

## 21. AI And Board Authoring

The Chronicle uses three live seats without pretending that the autonomous
civilizations are Architects.

### Myria policy

The Myriad policy should favor:

- adaptive, ecological, memory-preserving, and distributed capabilities;
- legal Forges that improve continuity under `coordination` and
  `transformation` pressure;
- ordinary denial only when it follows from shared Forge competition;
- no Encrypt action.

### Vesper policy

The Vesper policy should favor:

- timing, signal, predictive, energetic, and fast-coordination capabilities;
- legal Forges that improve response speed and phase coherence;
- ordinary denial only when it follows from shared Forge competition;
- no Encrypt action.

Neither policy chooses the player's coordination architecture. Neither reads
sealed campaign dimensions, hidden future outcomes, or the player's pending UI
selection.

The AI runner must safely execute two consecutive autonomous turns when needed,
stop immediately for the human turn or held Alignment, and remain bounded
against no-action loops.

The deterministic opening must:

- preserve normal costs and shared-card competition;
- make at least one preparedness-capable path reasonably available;
- avoid guaranteeing the Architect a dominant strategy;
- permit each reference civilization to win under legal play;
- avoid an opening in which two autonomous seats consume every meaningful
  choice before the Architect's second turn.

Exact order and weights are balance constants, not narrative canon.
