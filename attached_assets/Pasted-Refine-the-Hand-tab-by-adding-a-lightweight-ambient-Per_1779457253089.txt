The scene should show the player’s civilization scale based on forged Artifacts. It should feel like the player is watching their civilization grow from local sky → planetary civilization → star-system civilization → galactic civilization.

Do not change gameplay mechanics, card data, scoring, forging rules, artifact costs, tutorial flow, or navigation.

Placement:
- Add the scene at the top of the Hand tab only.
- It should not cover or obscure hand cards.
- It should be compact and responsive on phone width.
- It should feel like an atmospheric header, not a separate minigame.

Compute an internal Kardashev-style progress value from forged Artifacts:
- Tier I forged Artifact = 1 point
- Tier II forged Artifact = 3 points
- Tier III forged Artifact = 7 points

Map progress to ambient stages:
