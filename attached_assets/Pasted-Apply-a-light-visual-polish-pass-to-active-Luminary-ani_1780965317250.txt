Apply a light visual polish pass to active Luminary animation configs.

Goal:
Each Luminary should feel distinct while still using shared animation primitives:
- luminaryPulse
- targetClaim
- KeywordEvents
- Burn
- Condemned
- Forgotten
- Nullified
- Seeded
- scoreChange
- crystalReturn
- deckScry
- pendingAction
- marketRedraw

Do not create bespoke animation systems.
Do not change mechanics, costs, scoring, turn order, tutorial order, or card data.

Polish rules:
- Keep source, target, and result readable.
- Keep animations brief.
- Respect reduced-motion mode.
- Respect skip/fast-forward behavior.
- Do not reintroduce shrink-in/collapse-in generic activation.
- Use each Luminary’s existing color palette.
- Use flourish as overlay/motion flavor only.

Apply these identity flourishes:

1. Red Moth
- Red wing-shadow sweeps over targeted market cards.
- Burn fracture should feel like the card is split into before/after.

2. Tide Architect
- Deck tops ripple like tidewater.
- Continuum-biased cards surface through pale blue-white shimmer.

3. Verdant Oracle
- Persistent Verdance aura should feel like quiet root growth.
- Avoid explosive growth; it should feel patient and living.

4. Void Warden
- Board-wide dark ripple should feel silent and watchful.
- Score loss should feel like Eminence being swallowed by distance.

5. Concordance Mandala
- On threshold trigger, Radiance artifacts briefly align into geometric mandala light.
- Once-used marker should be small and elegant.

6. Phoenix Paradox
- Burned cards leave a blue-red recurrence afterimage.
- Surviving Flare/Continuum card should lock into place with a paradox pulse.

7. Catalyst Bloom
- Prior Burn cinders become green growth sparks.
- Do not replay all prior Burns unless necessary.

8. Iron Harbinger
- Hammer-shadow falls over Tier III row before Burn.
- Keep mass Burn readable and not too slow.

9. The Hourless Compass
- Compass needle spins once.
- A broken hour-ring passes over the market.
- Forgotten marker fades Eminence icons into blue-black haze.

10. Seed Beyond Seasons
- Blue-green seed glyphs plant onto deck tops.
- When a seeded card appears, the seed marker should quietly wake.

11. Glass Orchard
- Replication shimmer should feel like glass fruit refracting the copied Artifact.
- Keep copied-card movement clear.

12. Pale Merchant
- Pale scales briefly appear over overloaded crystal pools.
- Returned crystals should visibly move back to bank.

13. Ember Sovereign
- Condemned cards receive violet-orange mandate seals.
- Delayed Burn should feel like the sentence coming due.

14. First Hunger
- Assimilate replacement action should feel predatory but not like a bonus action.
- On use, selected Artifact Burns using shared Burn animation with hunger-colored accent fragments.
- Keep “Burn” terminology in UI/logs.

15. Null Sovereign
- Nullified cards receive cold black-white domain seals.
- Eminence icons should feel silenced, not destroyed.

Acceptance criteria:
- All Luminaries feel more distinct.
- All animations still use shared primitives.
- Reduced-motion and skip behavior still work.
- Source/target/result clarity is preserved.
- No mechanics are changed.
- Existing tests/typecheck pass.
