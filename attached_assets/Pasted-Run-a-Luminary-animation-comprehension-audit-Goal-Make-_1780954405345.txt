Run a Luminary animation comprehension audit.

Goal:
Make sure every Luminary animation clearly communicates:
1. Source — which Luminary caused this?
2. Target — what card/player/zone was affected?
3. Keyword/result — burn, forgotten, nullified, condemned, seeded, score loss/gain, crystal return, or pending action.
4. Aftermath — what changed on the board?

Do not change mechanics, costs, scoring, turn order, tutorial order, or card data.

For each Luminary, verify:
- The Luminary source visibly pulses first.
- The affected target is highlighted before the effect resolves.
- The keyword/result animation is distinct.
- The board state after animation matches the mechanical result.
- Any persistent state has a visible marker.
- Any temporary state clears visibly when expired.
- The animation does not obscure card names/costs/Eminence long enough to confuse play.
- The animation does not use generic shrink-in/collapse-in motion.

Specific checks:
- Burn cards visibly move toward or register with the Burn Pile.
- Forgotten and Nullified both suppress Eminence visually, but Nullified feels colder/more final.
- Condemned clearly reads as “will burn later.”
- Seeded remains trackable when the seeded card appears.
- Assimilate reads as a replacement core action, not a bonus action.
- Void Warden shows all players losing Eminence, including the summoner.
- Catalyst Bloom shows Eminence gain based on prior Burn events without replaying all burns.
