Update Ember Sovereign / Cinder Mandate.

Goal:
Ember Sovereign’s effect should read and behave as a two-stage sovereign judgment: first it marks unworthy artifacts as Condemned, then at the start of the owner’s next turn it burns the remaining condemned artifacts.

Update effect wording everywhere Ember Sovereign is displayed, including card text, tooltip text, Luminary detail modal, activation cinematic text, logs, and any reference/help text.

New canonical effect text:
“On arrival, mark each face-up Artifact as Condemned unless its forge cost includes 3 or more Flare, Abyss, or Radiance. At the start of your next turn, burn each remaining Condemned Artifact and refill its market slot.”

Rules clarification:

* A face-up Artifact survives Cinder Mandate if its forge cost includes at least one of the following:
    * 3 or more Flare
    * 3 or more Abyss
    * 3 or more Radiance
* If it does not satisfy at least one of those thresholds, mark it Condemned.
* Condemned cards do not burn immediately.
* Condemned cards burn at the start of the Ember Sovereign owner’s next turn.
* If a Condemned card is forged, reserved, removed, or otherwise leaves the market before that start-of-turn burn, do not burn it.
* After condemned cards burn, refill their market slots normally.
* Do not change Ember Sovereign’s ID, cost, affinities, Eminence value, art assets, or ownership logic unless strictly necessary for this effect.

Animation Phase 1 — Arrival Condemnation:
After the Living Entity breaks out of crystal and the player voluntarily exits the Luminary Presentation screen, continue the animation chain instead of ending immediately.

Sequence:

1. Keep camera/control locked.
2. Shrink the Ember Sovereign living entity into its vortex panel.
3. Center the camera on the board and switch to compact/full-board mode if necessary so all market targets are visible.
4. Play Ember Sovereign’s effect activation animation and sound.
5. Energy beams rain down from above onto every Artifact being marked Condemned.
6. Each impacted card receives a large, clear Condemned brand over most of the card face.
7. The brand pulses briefly, then resolves into the persistent Condemned marker/badge.
8. Restore the previous camera/view mode.
9. Resume normal gameplay.

Animation Phase 2 — Start-of-next-turn Judgment:
At the start of the Ember Sovereign owner’s next turn, before normal player actions:

1. Take control of the camera again.
2. Center/switch to compact or full-board view so all remaining Condemned cards are visible.
3. Play a shorter Ember Sovereign effect activation animation and sound.
4. Pulse all existing Condemned markers.
5. Apply the burn animation to all remaining Condemned cards.
6. Refill their market slots after the burn animation.
7. Restore the previous camera/view mode.
8. Continue the turn normally.

Marker / tooltip update:
Condemned marker tooltip should say:
“Condemned — Burns at the start of Ember Sovereign’s next turn.”

Visual requirements:

* Condemned marks must be large and unmistakable.
* Use Ember Sovereign colors: ember orange, red, black-violet, and sovereign gold.
* Energy beams should feel like judgment from above: sharp, controlled, royal, not chaotic lightning.
* Avoid blur-heavy effects.
* Prefer crisp beams, opacity, scale, masks, glow borders, and lightweight particles.
* The player should be able to trace the Condemned marker back to Ember Sovereign.

Reduced-motion / abridged mode:
Skip entity flourish and beam travel.
Use board-center camera, source pulse, target border flash, instant Condemned brand pop-in, and later a quick burn fade.

Validation:

* Add or update tests for the new Cinder Mandate threshold logic.
* Confirm artifacts with 3+ Flare survive.
* Confirm artifacts with 3+ Abyss survive.
* Confirm artifacts with 3+ Radiance survive.
* Confirm artifacts with only 1–2 Flare/Abyss/Radiance are Condemned.
* Confirm artifacts with no Flare/Abyss/Radiance are Condemned.
* Confirm Condemned cards burn at the start of the owner’s next turn, not immediately.
* Confirm cards removed before the next-turn burn are not burned again.
* Confirm market slots refill after the burn.
