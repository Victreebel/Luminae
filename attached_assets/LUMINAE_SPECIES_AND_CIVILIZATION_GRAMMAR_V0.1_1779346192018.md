# LUMINAE_SPECIES_AND_CIVILIZATION_GRAMMAR_V0.1.md

## Purpose

This document is a companion to `LUMINAE_SYSTEM_GRAMMAR_V0.1.md`.

It maps **Affinity combinations** into possible species, civilizations, artifact families, and Luminary directions for Luminae.

This document is not meant to rigidly define species taxonomy. It is a **worldbuilding and design generator**.

Core rule:

```text
Affinity combinations describe survival strategies, not fixed species types.
```

Correct:

```text
Verdance + Abyss = hidden adaptive life.
```

Incorrect:

```text
Verdance + Abyss = always fungi.
```

A single color combination should generate a **family of possibilities**, not one required species.

---

# 1. Core Grammar

A civilization/species path can be read as:

```text
Primary Affinity = what survives
Secondary Affinity = how it survives
Tertiary Affinity = what it becomes under pressure
```

Examples:

```text
Verdance + Abyss
= life that survives through concealment.
```

```text
Verdance + Abyss + Continuum
= life that survives through concealment and deep memory.
```

Artifacts are the technologies produced by these survival paths.
Luminaries are the mythic endpoints or destinies of these paths.

# 5. Singularity-Adjacent Civilization Concepts

Singularity is not a normal civilization affinity and should not create standard Singularity Artifacts or normal Singularity Luminary paths in the core system.

However, Singularity can describe convergence pressure or exceptional states.

Use Singularity for:

```text
wild substitution
compression
category collapse
impossible convergence
transcendent pressure
rule-bending moments
```

Examples:

```text
Verdance under Singularity pressure = self-redesigning life / evolutionary convergence.
Continuum under Singularity pressure = time-loop societies / recursive causal archives.
Abyss under Singularity pressure = event-horizon minds / unknowable void convergence.
Radiance under Singularity pressure = civilizations becoming universal symbols or laws.
Flare under Singularity pressure = unstable reactor gods / ascension through collapse.
```

Design warning:

```text
Singularity-adjacent ideas should feel rare, strange, and exceptional. They should not become a sixth ordinary color lane.
```

# 6. How To Use This Document

When designing a new card, ask:

```text
1. What survival strategy does this card express?
2. Is it mono-color, two-color, or three-color?
3. What kind of civilization would naturally build it?
4. What species or social form would live under that strategy?
5. What Luminary or destiny might it point toward?
6. Does its art reflect the correct survival logic?
```

For Artifacts:

```text
Artifact = technology produced by a survival path.
```

For Luminaries:

```text
Luminary = mythic endpoint of a survival path.
```

For Blueprints:

```text
Blueprint = strategic system or engine that teaches a civilization how to organize its technologies.
```

---

# 7. What Not To Do

Do not make affinity combinations rigid species definitions.

Do not make Verdance equal all biological life.

Do not make Abyss equal evil.

Do not make Radiance equal generic goodness.

Do not make Flare equal only fire.

Do not make Continuum equal only time magic.

Do not make Singularity a normal artifact color.

Do not force every possible combination into the base roster.

Do not redesign current Luminaries automatically because this grammar exists.

The useful shorthand:

```text
Flare = burn
Verdance = grow
Continuum = endure
Abyss = conceal
Radiance = reveal
Singularity = converge
```

Combination grammar:

```text
Primary = what survives
Secondary = how it survives
Tertiary = what it becomes under pressure
```
