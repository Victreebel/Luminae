LUMINAe — Luminary Mechanics Update v0.8

Goal:
Update the Luminary roster, Luminary timing rules, and Luminary effect logic exactly as specified below.

Do not infer additional mechanics.
Do not change Artifact IDs, Artifact costs, Artifact Eminence values, Artifact bonus affinities, Blueprint behavior, turn structure, UI layout, tutorial flow, or unrelated systems.
Do not rename anything not listed here.
Use “burn” in card text, but implement burn as refresh: remove the affected face-up market card and refill that market slot from the same deck.

GLOBAL RULES

1. Burn

* “Burn” means refresh the affected face-up Artifact.
* Animation should show destruction/ignition/erasure, then replacement/refill.
* A burn effect counts as one burn effect even if it burns multiple cards.

2. Scry

* “Scry X” means the player privately looks at the top X cards of the specified deck.
* Scry is not public reveal.
* Other players should not see the cards.

3. Reveal

* “Reveal” means public reveal.
* Use reveal only when specified.

4. Delayed Luminary Eminence

* Resolve all delayed Luminary Eminence before checking victory conditions.
* Delayed effects check at the end of the owning player’s turn unless otherwise specified.

5. Marked Cards

* Forgotten, Condemned, Nullified, and Avatar Seed are visible markers.
* If a marked card is forged while its marker applies, use the marked rule.
* If a marked card is burned/removed/refreshed before being forged, remove its marker unless otherwise specified.
