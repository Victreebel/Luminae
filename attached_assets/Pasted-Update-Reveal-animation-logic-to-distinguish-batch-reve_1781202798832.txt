Update Reveal animation logic to distinguish batch reveal from sequential reveal-until effects.

Goal:
Reveal animations should match the structure of the effect. “Reveal 6” is not the same as “Reveal up to 6 until you reveal X.”

Do not change gameplay mechanics, costs, scoring, turn order, tutorial order, or balance.

Definitions:

1. Batch Reveal
    Used for effects worded like:

* “Reveal 6 cards.”
* “Reveal the top N cards.”
* “Reveal N, then burn all cards matching X.”
* “Reveal N, choose/keep/burn based on criteria.”

Animation behavior:

* Smoothly move to Compact View / full-board framing if needed.
* Cards are revealed one by one.
* Each revealed card flips forward into the forefront / inspection row.
* Revealed cards stay visible in the forefront until the full reveal batch is complete.
* After all cards are revealed, apply the effect outcome:
    * Cards being Burned should Burn together or in a fast staggered batch.
    * Cards being kept should fly/shrink/slide to their final slot/location.
    * Cards being returned should fly/shrink/slide back to deck or appropriate zone.
    * Cards being marked should receive the appropriate marker animation.
* The player should understand the whole revealed set before outcomes resolve.

Example:
“Reveal 6, then Burn all Flare cards.”
Animation:
Reveal card 1 → hold in forefront.
Reveal card 2 → hold in forefront.
Reveal card 3 → hold in forefront.
Reveal card 4 → hold in forefront.
Reveal card 5 → hold in forefront.
Reveal card 6 → hold in forefront.
Then target all revealed Flare cards → Burn them together or in a quick readable stagger.
Non-Burned cards fly/shrink to their final locations.

2. Sequential Reveal-Until
    Used for effects worded like:

* “Reveal up to 6 cards until you reveal a Flare card.”
* “Reveal cards one by one until X is revealed.”
* “Reveal until a matching card is found.”

Animation behavior:

* Smoothly move to Compact View / full-board framing if needed.
* Reveal one card at a time.
* Each card flips forward into the forefront.
* Immediately evaluate that card.
* If the card fails the condition:
    * Apply its fate immediately, usually Burn, discard, return, or move.
    * Then proceed to the next reveal.
* If the card satisfies the condition:
    * Hold it briefly in the forefront.
    * Emphasize that it matched.
    * Then animate it flying/shrinking/sliding to its final current location.
    * Stop revealing.
* Do not reveal all cards first for reveal-until effects.
* The suspense should come from the one-by-one check.

Example:
“Reveal up to 6 cards until you reveal a card with Flare.”
Animation:
Reveal card 1 → if not Flare, Burn/resolve it → reveal card 2.
Reveal card 2 → if not Flare, Burn/resolve it → reveal card 3.
Continue until a Flare card appears.
When Flare appears → pulse/lock matching card → move it to final location → stop.

3. Forefront reveal presentation

* Revealed cards should flip one by one into a clear forefront/inspection position.
* Forefront cards should be larger or visually elevated enough to be readable.
* Do not blur.
* Do not obscure card name, affinity, cost, or Eminence.
* Use crisp flip/slide/scale motion.
* When resolution is complete, cards should visibly meet their fate:
    * Burned cards use the shared bottom-up Burn animation.
    * Kept cards fly/shrink/slide into their current market/location.
    * Returned cards fly/shrink/slide back to the deck.
    * Marked cards receive the appropriate marker/Arrival Brand animation.

4. Phoenix Paradox / Ash-Seeking Recurrence
    Phoenix Paradox should use Sequential Reveal-Until behavior, not Batch Reveal.

Animation:

* Luminary pulse.
* Compact View / affected market framing if needed.
* Reveal/check cards one by one from Tier III then Tier II according to existing mechanics.
* Each nonmatching card resolves immediately through shared Burn animation.
* Continue until a Flare or Continuum card is revealed.
* The matching card pulses red-blue, stays, and moves/settles into its face-up market slot.
* Stop the loop.

5. Event log wording
    Batch reveal:
    “[Source] revealed [N] cards.”
    Then:
    “[N] revealed cards were Burned / moved / returned / kept.”

Sequential reveal-until:
“[Source] revealed cards one by one until [condition].”
For each failed card:
“[Card Name] did not match and was Burned.”
For the match:
“[Card Name] matched and remains.”

Visual rules:

* No blur.
* No abrupt cuts.
* No generic shrink-in/collapse-in.
* Cards may shrink/fly only when moving to their actual destination.
* Keep revealed cards readable.
* Batch outcomes resolve after the full batch is shown.
* Reveal-until outcomes resolve one by one.

Acceptance criteria:

* Batch Reveal and Sequential Reveal-Until are distinct animation procedures.
* Batch Reveal holds all revealed cards in the forefront before resolving outcomes.
* Sequential Reveal-Until reveals and resolves cards one by one.
* Phoenix Paradox uses Sequential Reveal-Until.
* Burned revealed cards use the shared bottom-up Burn animation.
* Cards clearly fly/shrink/slide to their actual destination after reveal resolution.
* Existing tests/typecheck pass.
