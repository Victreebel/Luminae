# Luminae — Luminary Reference Guide

All 12 Luminaries currently in the game pool. Eminence rewards are paid to the summoning player only, unless marked **Oblivion** (dealt to all players).

**Affinity key:**
Flare (🔴 ruby) · Continuum (🔵 sapphire) · Verdance (🟢 emerald) · Abyss (⚫ onyx) · Radiance (⚪ pearl)

**Living Luminary bonus:** Once claimed, the owner gains **+1 discount** toward the marked affinity on every card purchase, starting the turn after summoning. Multi-affinity Luminaries let the owner toggle which affinity is active (cycling with a tap).

## Game Rules — Luminaries

- At game start, **playerCount + 1** Luminaries are randomly drawn from the pool and placed face-up.
- A player claims a Luminary by spending the exact affinities from their crystal supply on their turn (no leftover crystal payment — requirements are spent directly from crystals held, not purchasing power).
- Each Luminary is claimed **once** — first to meet requirements takes it.
- **Oblivion Luminaries** (Void Warden, Null Sovereign) subtract Eminence from all players simultaneously on the turn they are summoned.
- The **Living Luminary bonus** activates on the summoner's *next* turn and persists for the rest of the game.
- Bonus affinity can be toggled at any time, even off-turn (costs no action).
