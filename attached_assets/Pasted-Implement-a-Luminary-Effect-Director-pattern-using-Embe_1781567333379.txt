Implement a Luminary Effect Director pattern using Ember Sovereign / Cinder Mandate as the reference implementation.

Problem:
Cinder Mandate is currently assembled from reusable pieces — arrival cutscene, activation cinematic, view orchestrator, brand strikes, marker overlays, BurnFlash, market redraw — but too many of those pieces behave like independent mini-directors. This creates awkward camera behavior, rough compact/restore transitions, disconnected activation beats, and confusion between branding and burn phases.

Goal:
Keep the reusable animation primitives, but give each Luminary activation event one top-level director that owns sequencing, camera authority, marker visibility, and animation completion.

Architecture rule:
Each pending Luminary activation event should be routed to one director/procedure. That director owns:

* camera/input lock during the effect
* whether Compact View is entered
* whether Compact View is restored or preserved
* source pulse timing
* target claim timing
* brand strike or burn primitive calls
* marker suppression/unsuppression
* BurnFlash timing
* Burn Pile update timing
* market redraw timing
* aftermath hold
* animationEndTime/drain gate release
* resolve_luminary_activation

Reusable primitives should not independently:

* call viewOrchestrator.prepare() a second time
* restore the camera unless requested by the director
* release the animation drain gate
* trigger BurnFlash outside the director timeline
* trigger brand strikes outside the director timeline
* resolve the activation event

Implement two Cinder Mandate directors:

1. CinderMandateBrandingDirector
    Triggered by:

* luminaryId: “lum_ember”
* effectType: “summon”

Purpose:
Introduce the Condemned state visually.

Sequence:

1. Runs only after LuminaryArrivalCutscene has completed.
2. Locks camera/input with a light cinematic authority layer.
3. Performs one viewOrchestrator.prepare() to frame Ember Sovereign source/portal and all Condemned market targets.
4. Enters or remains in Compact View.
5. Board dims slightly with Ember/violet atmosphere.
6. Shows a short activation beat: “CINDER MANDATE”.
7. Ember Sovereign source/portal pulses.
8. Condemned target claim rings appear.
9. Brand strike beams fire to every target from the event payload.
10. Large ⌬ impact glyph appears on each target.
11. Persistent Condemned badge/vignette appears exactly when each beam lands.
12. After all strikes, hold the board briefly so players can inspect the newly Condemned market.
13. Remain in Compact View after the sequence. Do not auto-restore to normal view.
14. Release camera/input and resolve the activation.

Important:

* This director must not trigger BurnFlash.
* It must use the server-provided targetCardIds payload, not live-state rediscovery as the primary target source.
* If a beam/DOM target fails, unsuppress any remaining payload target IDs at the end so rules and visuals do not diverge.

2. CinderMandateBurnDirector
    Triggered by:

* luminaryId: “lum_ember”
* effectType: “end_of_turn”

Purpose:
Execute the delayed burn at the end of Ember Sovereign owner’s next turn.

Sequence:

1. Locks camera/input with the same light cinematic authority layer.
2. Briefly darkens the board with ember/violet vignette.
3. Shows a short decree interstitial, about 1.5–2.0 seconds:
    * title: “CINDER MANDATE”
    * optional subtitle: “The condemned are burned.”
    * board remains faintly visible behind the overlay if possible.
4. Frames all remaining condemned burn targets in Compact View.
5. Condemned cards pulse/shudder for 350–500ms.
6. A short heat wash crosses the viewport.
7. BurnFlash plays bottom-up on every remaining target:
    * crisp flame line from bottom to top
    * no blur
    * no generic fade-out
    * card remains readable early in the burn
8. Burn Pile updates.
9. Market redraw happens after the burn animation.
10. Hold the aftermath briefly.
11. Prefer remaining in Compact View after the sequence, especially if multiple cards burned.
12. Release camera/input and resolve the activation.

Important:

* This director must not trigger brand strikes.
* This director should use the event payload for burn targets.
* If targetCardIds alone is insufficient to locate old slots after market refill, add a richer payload such as burnedTargets with cardId, tier, slotIndex, and card snapshot.
* No Cinder Mandate burn should occur or animate at start_of_turn in new games.

Cinder Mandate timing rule:

* Summon event = branding / Condemned marker introduction.
* End-of-turn event = Burn execution.
* Start-of-turn should not visibly animate Cinder Mandate burn in new games.

Camera authority:
Major Luminary effects should temporarily own camera/input like a lighter version of summoning:

* disable manual view toggles during the directed sequence
* prevent scroll/view interference during the sequence if applicable
* suppress competing UI overlays if they clutter the effect
* release only after the director’s aftermath hold completes

Acceptance criteria:

* Ember Sovereign arrival still plays normally.
* Cinder Mandate branding happens after arrival, not during the next turn.
* Branding uses one unified timeline and one viewOrchestrator.prepare().
* Every Condemned target from the payload gets struck or at least unsuppressed by the end.
* Condemned markers persist visibly through the waiting window.
* Cinder Mandate burn happens only at end_of_turn.
* Burn sequence has a brief decree beat before BurnFlash.
* Burn uses bottom-up BurnFlash, Burn Pile update, and market redraw.
* No brand strikes during burn.
* No BurnFlash during summon branding.
* No automatic rough restore after Cinder Mandate branding.
* Camera/input cannot be manually disrupted during the directed effect.
* Existing tests/typecheck pass.
